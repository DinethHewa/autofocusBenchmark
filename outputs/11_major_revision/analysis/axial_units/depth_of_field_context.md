# Depth-of-field engineering context

The source acquisition paper for the PBS, BMA, and TBF stacks reports a
100×/1.4 NA oil-immersion objective, a 0.5 µm axial sampling interval, and an
approximate 0.5 µm depth of field under its stated green-light assumption
(Manescu et al., *Biomedical Optics Express* 13, 1005, 2022;
https://doi.org/10.1364/BOE.448280). Thus, the source-reported depth of field is
approximately one sampled interval for those three domains.

This value is used only as an engineering-scale acquisition context. It is not
a measured clinical tolerance, an independent focus ground truth, or evidence
that a one-slice error is clinically acceptable. No depth-of-field value is
assigned to WBC or TBI because their required optical parameters are not
verified from the available provenance.
