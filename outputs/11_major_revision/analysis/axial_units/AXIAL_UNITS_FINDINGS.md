# Acquisition metadata and axial-unit findings

Axial errors must first be reported in slices. Micrometre conversion is made only with a verified domain-specific step: WBC 0.4 µm; PBS, BMA and TBF 0.5 µm; and the official TBIMAGE_DB_FOCUS.V1 record reports 2.5 µm. No pooled five-domain slice error is multiplied by a single domain's spacing.

The actual cached dimensions are 200 × 200 (WBC), 2112 × 2816 (TBI), and 2160 × 2560 (PBS/BMA/TBF). Pixel size is not reported in the verified records and is not inferred from camera specifications. Depth-of-field claims are therefore not used to assert localization saturation. Missing metadata are written as “not reported.”
